The Tale Keeper

The Tale Keeper is an English-language YouTube channel of narrated stories from history, myth and legend.

Watch on YouTube: @TheTaleKeeperTV

"The Tale Keeper Publisher" is the channel's own internal tool. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload and schedule the channel's videos and the YouTube Analytics API to read the channel's performance reports. It is used only by the channel owner and accesses no other users' data.
